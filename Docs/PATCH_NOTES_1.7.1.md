# Rift Crown Arena 1.7.1 — Swarm Combat Hotfix

Swarm troops now keep steadier attack positions instead of continually shuffling around their targets. Friendly troops can make room for one another; opposing troops hold their combat positions.

Overhead slow percentage text and VEIL labels are removed. Slow and cloak abilities, their animations and visual effects remain active. Other battle UI is unchanged.

Every card and subordinate unit keeps its 1.7.0 stats and abilities. There are no changes to Aether costs, HP, damage, attack intervals, movement speeds, attack ranges, projectiles, collision radii, model scales, models or card art. The arena remains 40 tiles long by 22 tiles wide.

Navigation rules revision advances from 9 to 10, so new match, replay and Meta data identifies the corrected rules.
