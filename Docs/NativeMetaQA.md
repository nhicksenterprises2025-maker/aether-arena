The dataset is an aggregate of simulated generated decks, not a per-match snapshot archive. The worker validates each final bank/spend/leak budget and finite terminal state; the independent aggregate audit checks the retained totals and exported rows. Card/pair win rates describe associations within decks and opposing styles. Mirror exclusion helps interpretation but does not turn shared match/deck observations into independent card experiments; Wilson intervals are descriptive. Archetypes overlap, so their pick rates need not sum to 100%. Active simulation wall seconds omit pause/throttle waits and aggregation/JSON/persistence overhead; they do not measure frame rate, total elapsed runtime or player UI performance. Future runtime QA also supplies an isolated `-UserDir` for engine settings alongside the isolated `-RiftSaveRoot` for native profile/replay/dataset data.
