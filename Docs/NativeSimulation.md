# Authoritative native simulation

`RiftSimulation.h` and five files in `Private/Simulation` implement portable C++20 gameplay shared by the UE game, Meta worker and native regression executable. They include no Unreal/render dependencies. UE owns presentation, input mapping, profile/replay persistence, dataset aggregation and UI. The inspection contract is `UE5_PARITY_SPEC.md` in the repository root.

Construct `rift::Match` with `MatchOptions` containing seed, optional validated eight-card decks, each team's AI enable flag and seven-style names. Empty decks select a coherent AI deck or the player default. `State()` returns a serialization-friendly snapshot. Call `Step(simulationSeconds)`; it accumulates exact 1/60-second steps, so host pause passes zero and host speed multiplies elapsed input. Rendering must interpolate snapshots without mutating the state. The same seed and timestamped actions produce the same match regardless of frame subdivision. No per-frame RNG enters authoritative state.

Use `Play(team, handIndex, {x,z}, reason)` for paid deployment. The method validates the board/bank/pocket/full building footprint and bank, spends and cycles exactly once, and records a stable paid cast ID before spawning all members/effects. `Spawn` is tagged free DEV deployment: no spend or hand rotation. `SetAether`, `SetTowerHP`, `ClearField`, `SetAIEnabled` and `SetAIStyle` are recorded developer actions. Invalid/nonfinite input and postresult/tiebreaker deployment return false. Team indices are Player0 and Enemy1; Player resides at positive Z. One tile can be mapped to100 UE cm.

The roster in `Cards()` is the frozen complete V15 mechanical source:14 exact identifiers, stats, swarm counts, special values and collision scales. Native DataAssets must be validated against it. `AnalyzeDeck`, `CounterScore`/`CounterReason`, `PairSynergy`/`PairSynergyReasons`, `IntelligenceFor` and `BuildAIDeck` reuse the same library; there is no separate balance estimator roster. Archetype classifications overlap. Counter and synergy scores are mechanic estimates, never observed win probabilities. Card intelligence includes the original roles, use advice, four best/weak answers, four partners and defensive/offensive recommendations.

Normal targeting uses front8/rear5 sight and temporary deployment pulls; Crown hard lock starts upon legal attack-range entry before a first swing. Structure-only units can lock defensive buildings too. Core activation occurs only after destruction of a friendly Guard, correcting the browser headless-only on-hit activation discrepancy. Projectiles retain source/entity/cast attribution; DOT's fifth tick is inclusive. Frost refreshes nonstacking movement slow; Raven aura damages/stuns only nearby troops and remains separate from its direct structure attacks. Buildings decay while fighting. All ordinary damage is actual removed HP, with overkill recorded separately.

Ground movement uses bounded radius-aware A* with no corner cutting, living structure obstacles, forced bridge commitments, congestion costs, stable crossing slots for fallback, repathing, legal lateral correction, averaged soft separation and stuck probes. Flying movement bypasses it. A* uses a29×43 flat grid and cached per-query obstacle/congestion lists to avoid thousands of tree-node allocations. Helper globals live in individual translation units; module `bUseUnity=false` prevents unity merging of independent helpers.

The clock implements180-second regulation and only0–0 overtime120 seconds, first OT tower/Core win, then frozen-combat180HP/s tiebreaker after .85s. Nonzero tied regulation draws. Exact simultaneous TB is decided from initial minimumHP, initial totalHP, then seeded coin; only the losing side's fallen Crown is recorded and final score1–0. `AetherGenerated(from,to)` integrates1×/2×/3× boundaries at120/240 seconds. Economy is capped10 with capacity leakage. Like browser update ordering, the terminal clock transition can stop the last economy tick: recorded spend+bank+leak is at most theoretical budget and differs by less than .04 Aether, rather than inventing free resources.

`Events()` preserves monotonic sequence/time; `DrainEvents()` transfers ownership once. Event types cover match start, card play, spawn, attack/projectile, damage/death, status, pulls, aura/hazard ticks, tower destruction/Core activation, economy/DEV, AI decision, phase and result. Store raw events and snapshots for replay; rendering a seek must not call current AI/RNG. Initial Crown state is in the initial snapshot. Do not attribute DEV edits, decay or TB drain to a card attack.

Telemetry distinguishes plays, swarm spawns, actual troop/tower/building damage, taken HP, member kills/deaths/value, censored survivor lifetime, paid bank, unique cast targets, connection, initial/DOT/aura damage, overkill, placement, openings, first/OT casts, structure pulls and Crown damage contribution. Building capacity is25seconds per spawn; actual building lifetime integrates living time. Opening eligibility counts opening-hand presence. Slow/stun have distinct affected seconds and remaining-target exposure denominators: exposure is settled before current attacks refresh source ownership, preventing order-dependent double or stale credit. Zone occupancy is integrated occupants×seconds divided by active zone seconds. Root Meta aggregation supplies appearance/clean-N denominators, Wilson/prior statistics, archives and filters.

AI decisions consume only own deck/hand/bank, visible entities/crowns and observed opposing paid plays. Opposing bank is estimated from visible spending and regeneration; known counter readiness uses the most recent observed occurrence plus four later plays. Seven styles share legal deployment/payment but vary bank/reserve/support/defense/punish/trade policies, priority lists, pocket pressure, finish spells, counterpush, split, late lead holds and desperation. Deck generation enumerates all legal14-choose8 compositions, applies coverage/style constraints, samples a narrow top score band with the seed, and shuffles independently; observed win rate never biases deck population.
