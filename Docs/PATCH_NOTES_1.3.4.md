# Rift Crown Arena 1.3.4

The arena is now 30 by 44 tiles, adding one tile on every side. Both teams' Core and Guard Towers move one tile toward their own rear. The bridges keep their current positions.

Ground troops cross the bridge on their current side of the arena and hold that choice while crossing. When their lane's Guard Tower has fallen, they advance toward the Core instead of switching to the surviving Guard on the other side. Nearby enemy troops and buildings still draw appropriate aggro; tower attack locks and continuous body clearance remain active.

Meta Lab now has a **Stats Guide** explaining sample counts, pick rates, raw and adjusted win rates, confidence intervals, damage efficiency and all report types. Simulated deck performance and rule-based mechanical matchup scores are clearly described. New simulations use the updated arena and routing fingerprint; existing datasets remain available as history.

All fourteen cards, their costs and combat stats, troop counts, flying and spell behavior, drag-to-cancel controls, model portraits, typography, audio, training and replay tools are retained.
