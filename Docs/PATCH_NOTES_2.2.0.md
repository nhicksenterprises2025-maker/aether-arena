# Rift Crown Arena 2.2.0 — Plague Queen

Plague Queen joins the collection as a 2-Aether ground troop. Her green slime body, ornate crown and bow, animated toxic bubbles, and portrait use the new in-game model.

**Poison arrows:** Only the directly struck target takes the immediate 212 damage. Enemies within 6 tiles of the impact receive poison, including the direct target if it survives. Ground troops, flying troops and structures are eligible; friendly units are unaffected.

Poison deals 60 damage after 1.25 seconds and another 60 after 2.5 seconds. Maximum exposure from one arrow is **332 damage to its direct target** and **120 damage to each other poisoned enemy**, before shields, mitigation and remaining-HP limits. The 212 direct hit is never multiplied across the splash area.

Poison follows affected enemies when they move away. Entering the impact area later does not apply poison. Separate arrows apply separate two-tick effects. Existing poison still resolves if its Queen dies, with damage credited to her deployment. Invisibility does not grant immunity, and poison effects do not reveal cloaked enemies to opponents.

Arrows travel visibly at 30 tiles/s before impact. Toxic splashes mark the impact area, with attached poison effects showing affected visible enemies. Idle and moving Queens emit animated toxic bubbles.

Collection details, deck analysis, AI, replays, Meta Lab, in-game notes and spreadsheet exports include the new card. Existing card stats, arena layout, models and audio retain their 2.1.0 values.

Basic DPS is direct damage divided by attack interval. Poison is listed separately because damage depends on which enemies are caught and survive both ticks.
