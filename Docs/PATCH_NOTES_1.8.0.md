# Rift Crown Arena 1.8.0 — Forged in Aether

The full roster receives a coordinated model, animation, portrait and effects pass. Numerical balance remains unchanged from 1.7.1.

- Troops: clearer faces, distinct silhouettes, fitted equipment, refined armor and materials. Bone Warden's captive skeletons move inside the cage; Reaper's scythe and Warden's hammer retain deliberate grips.
- Creatures: distinct flight rhythms, staggered swarm gaits and secondary ear, mane and cloth motion. Mini Stampede remains scrappy; Stampede keeps its royal equipment.
- Buildings: readable mechanisms and attack motion. Pulverizer's purple pulse travels across the ground with its actual shockwave.
- Animation: smoother presentation between fixed simulation updates, with attack contact and spell impact timing preserved. Faster replay and training speeds keep safe authoritative positions.
- Attacks and abilities: restrained material-specific hit reactions, directional contact effects and clearer elemental windup and travel. Reaper retains its purple aura, cleave and one-second cloak at half health.
- Card art: portraits rendered from the updated production models, with consistent framing and lighting.
- Audio: three short deployment cue variations per playable card. A swarm plays one cue per deployment. Existing music and combat masters remain intact.
- Replays: deployment cues play correctly when opening, rewinding and reopening a recording; live swarm deployments retain duplicate suppression.
- Interface: carved metal frames, warm ivory type, quieter stone backgrounds, tactile buttons and larger hand portraits. Existing screens, statistics, replays, Meta Lab, settings and training controls remain available.
- Arena: richer grass, bridges, lighting and perimeter detail. The hand remains centered behind the Core Tower and outside the battlefield.

The battlefield remains **40 tiles long × 22 tiles wide**, with **125 cm tiles**. Tower and bridge alignment, damage-only health bars, drag-to-return cancellation, friendly yielding and opposing combat contact rules are preserved. Troop routing and swarm collision scenarios are verified alongside the presentation update.

All 21 playable cards and the subordinate skeleton retain their costs, counts, HP, damage, intervals, movement and projectile speeds, attack and detection ranges, abilities, collision radii and approved model scales. Basic DPS remains calculated from damage divided by attack interval. Balance identity: `88c28ff155ef5e63becb70515e0d30b1cef10c2d0f0b7cfc53ced371e4d9c90a`.
