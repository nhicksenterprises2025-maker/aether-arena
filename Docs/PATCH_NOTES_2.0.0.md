# Rift Crown Arena 2.0.0 — Roster detail and tower spacing

Both Guard Towers on each team move exactly one tile toward the Core Tower's centerline: horizontal centers change from ±7.76 to ±6.76 tiles. One tile remains 125 world units. Core Towers, tower depth, bridges, stone paths and the 40-by-22 arena retain their positions and dimensions.

Every other card receives model detail brought up to Dart Gun's visual standard. Human characters gain fitted clothing, armor, faces and weapon details; creatures gain species-specific anatomy, coats, wings and equipment. Archer Tower and Pulverizer receive structural and mechanical detail. Nova Flask, Meteor Shards, Bullet Burst and Shadow Surge receive more detailed spell geometry. Bone Warden's captive and summoned skeletons and Archer Tower's archer are included. Card portraits are rendered from these game models.

Dart Gun retains his accepted model and portrait. Existing animation rigs and gameplay timing remain intact; new details follow their corresponding joints and mechanisms. The roster remains 22 playable cards and one subordinate skeleton. All numerical card stats, model scales, abilities, targeting rules, collision radii and audio retain their 1.9.0 values.

Navigation revision 13 records the changed Guard Tower positions in new Meta simulations. Historical results keep their original rules. Spreadsheet-ready card, ability and summon tables are included for 2.0.0; their numerical values match 1.9.0.
