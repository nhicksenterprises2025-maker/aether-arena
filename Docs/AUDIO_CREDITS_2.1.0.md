download URLs, archive members and SHA-256 pins are preserved in

Deployment performances are edited, layered and mastered for each Rift Crown
character. Human calls use recorded acknowledgements, incantations and battle
cries. Creature signatures combine performed creature sounds with recorded
animals and material foley; hog calls are stylized creature performances.
No voice was cloned and no speech generation service output is included.

Existing music, combat cues and UI sounds remain the released 1.7.1 masters.
The score composition and cue arrangements are original to Rift Crown Arena.

Dart Gun adds five mastered cues: `spawn_dart_gun`, `spawn_dart_gun_v2`,
`spawn_dart_gun_v3`, `dart_fire` and `dart_hit`. His three deployment takes
use Brandon Song's recorded acknowledgements with restrained breath texture,
Tinysized leather handling and metal latch foley. Dart fire combines Tinysized
metal and arrow-feather recordings with original short air synthesis; dart
impact combines Kenney wood impact and Tinysized arrow-feather recordings.
These source recordings use the same CC0 palette credited above. All 126
previously released masters and their source metadata retain their exact bytes.

Thunderstorm and Blade Box add three short deployment variations each, plus
`thunder_strike` and `blade_release`. They combine original synthesized thunder,
air and chimes with the credited Tinysized twig, metal latch, knife scrape and
leather recordings. All eight cues are one-shots; there is no rain ambience loop.
All 131 previously released masters and their metadata retain their exact bytes.
