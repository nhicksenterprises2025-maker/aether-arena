# Rift Crown Arena 1.3.0

Revised character models and model-rendered card portraits make faces, equipment and troop identities easier to read.

- All fourteen cards have updated production models and matching portraits. Humanoid equipment uses coherent armor layers and deliberate weapon grips; creature silhouettes, faces and surface details are revised.
- Humanoid portraits frame the face and held equipment more closely. Each card uses lighting suited to its materials and identity.
- The game interface uses the bundled Barlow Semi Condensed font family, with separate weights for labels, body text and emphasis. The main layout is retained.
- Drag a card from your hand to a legal arena position and release to deploy. Drag it back to the hand, release over another UI panel or an invalid position, or cancel to keep it in your hand without paying Aether.
- Clicking to select and place, the 1–4 shortcuts, placement confirmation and pause controls remain available.

Meteor Shards and Bullet Burst retain their 0.75/0.30-second animated impact delays. The fourteen-card numerical combat rules, enlarged battlefield, centered hand, first-damage health bars, audio, replays and training tools remain available.
