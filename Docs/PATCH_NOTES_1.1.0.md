# Rift Crown Arena 1.1.0

This update rebuilds the interface around the battlefield, brings the Training tools back into view, and replaces the sound palette and music.

- The lobby, collection, card details, loadout workshop, profile and settings use a consistent layout, illustrated cards and clearer navigation. Your active deck and its coverage are visible from the lobby.
- The battle hand has framed card art, Aether costs, selection and affordability feedback, and a card preview that follows a drag. The next card has its own illustration. A ten-segment Aether meter, separate crown scores, phase timer and brief surge announcements make the match state easier to read.
- Escape opens a pause menu with resume, settings, loadout, the Field Manual, restart and exit. Resuming restores the previous simulation speed. Card deployment is blocked while the menu is open.
- Training shows live speed, Aether, AI state and tower health. Both teams have AI toggles; spawning, bank changes, tower edits and clearing the battlefield remain available. Paths, front/rear sight, attack ranges, targets, hard locks and tile coordinates now render in the Windows game.
- The new Field Manual explains deployment, paid card cycling, targeting, tower rules, match phases and card specials. Replay controls, key-moment jumps, event feed and match analysis have clearer layouts. Saved recordings can still be imported and exported.
- Fractional replay endings now include the final tower destruction and match result. Off-board clicks no longer snap into a legal edge deployment, and the selected-card inspector fits at the largest interface scale. Triple-Aether announcements remain visible during overtime.
- Combat uses recorded metal, wood, stone, cloth and air layers, with three takes for ten common cues. A new chamber-orchestra score, related match stingers, quiet tactile interface sounds and a water ambience replace the earlier synthesized palette. Repeated swarm sounds are grouped, important events keep a voice available, and announcements briefly lower the music. Master, Music, SFX and UI controls remain independent.

The fourteen-card roster and all numerical gameplay rules are preserved, including costs, damage, health, ranges, targeting, tower behavior, placement, paid cycling, Aether phases and match timing. Existing arena models, materials, scenery, animations and effects remain in place. This update does not reset your profile, deck presets, settings, replays or Meta datasets.

Controls: select a hand card or press 1–4, then click a legal tile or drag the card onto it. Right click cancels selection. Escape opens or closes the battle menu. Training tools are available through DEV during a battle.
