# Recorded audio palette

These source samples render the finished game sounds in `Build/generate_audio.py`.
All are CC0 1.0 / public domain. Music composition, cue layering, mastering and
arrangement are original to Rift Crown Arena. Upstream filenames, pinned URLs
and SHA-256 hashes are in `sources.json`. The game contains rendered SoundWaves.

- **Impact Sounds** — Kenney, https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- **Tinysized SFX** — Jan Schupke / Vehicle, https://opengameart.org/content/fantasy-sound-effects-tinysized-sfx
- **VSCO 2 Community Edition** — Versilian Studios, recorded by Sam Gossner and
  Simon Dalzell; sample cutting by Elan Hickler / Soundemote.
  https://versilian-studios.com/vsco-community/
  https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
