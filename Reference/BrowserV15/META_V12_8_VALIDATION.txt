Rift Crown Arena V12.8 — Meta Lab Validation

The Meta Lab remains an analytical balance simulator at ~100 games/minute. V12.8 resets the stored
meta dataset because the card pool and card mechanics changed materially.

New mechanic math included in V12.8:

1. Frost Fang
   - Raw DPS: 72 / 0.8 = 90.0 DPS.
   - Slow: 30% movement reduction, 2.0s refresh duration, non-stacking.
   - Because 2.0s duration exceeds the 0.8s hit interval, slow uptime after first contact is effectively 100%
     while Frost Fang stays connected to a ground target.
   - Meta counter math applies the movement-control value only to ground cards and weights the effect more
     strongly against structure-targeting ground win conditions, which lose more pressure when slowed.

2. Storm Raven
   - Direct structure DPS: 282 / 1.3 = 216.92 DPS.
   - Lightning ring: 82 / 3.0 = 27.33 raw area DPS while enemies remain within 2 tiles.
   - Stun duty cycle: 0.4 / 3.0 = 13.33% maximum local action denial.
   - Meta model adds aura damage and stun control to troop/defensive contribution while keeping direct attack
     pressure in the structure-only tower-pressure model.

3. Meteor Shards
   - Initial damage: 151.
   - Maximum stationary-target damage: 151 + (6 x 5) = 181.
   - Live gameplay applies 6 damage once per second only while the enemy troop remains inside the 4.5-tile zone.
   - The Meta Lab therefore does NOT assume all targets receive the full 181 damage. It estimates dwell time from
     target movement speed and spell radius and clamps it to the five-second zone duration.
   - Crown/building damage is 0 because this card's supplied targeting was Area Ground/Air troops.

Statistical checks retained from V12.3:
   - card win rate excludes mirror-card games for that card;
   - 24-game neutral Bayesian prior reduces tiny-sample extremes;
   - Wilson 95% confidence interval is shown for non-mirror samples;
   - deck pick rates do not feed back from observed win rate;
   - card use is constrained by the live Aether economy;
   - cards are sampled without duplicates inside an 8-card deck.

Pool sanity:
   - 14 cards live after V12.8.
   - 8 unique cards per deck.
   - Across a large unbiased sample, total Pick% across all cards should converge to ~800%, because every deck
     contains eight cards. Archetype weighting changes individual Pick%, not that total.

Important:
   - Meta Lab results are analytical projections, not rendered-battle telemetry. They are appropriate for finding
     likely balance outliers and matchup patterns, then should be confirmed against live battle results.

10,000-game deck-sampling sanity check (20,000 simulated deck drafts):
   - Total Pick% across the 14-card pool = exactly 800.0%.
   - Individual Pick% remained in a narrow ~55–59% band under the current three archetype weights.
   - This indicates the style weighting is influencing archetypes without starving any card of samples.
