RIFT CROWN ARENA V15 — META LAB 2.0 MATHEMATICAL AUDIT
Model: v15-event-2. Validated fingerprint: 47a565c8.

WHAT IS MEASURED

The new Meta Lab runs seeded headless battles in a Web Worker. Each match has
eight unique cards per side, a shuffled four-card hand, a queue, a real capped
Aether account, legal deployments, individual swarm members, towers, moving
troops, projectiles, spell areas, status effects and building decay. Outcomes
come from actual simulated HP loss, tower destruction and the live match rules.
Damage, deaths, spell occupancy, status time, uses and spending are accumulated
from those events. There is no Gaussian pressure score deciding the winner and
no independent random telemetry appended after an outcome is chosen.

The exact live CARD_LIBRARY, MATCH, GRID, ARENA, SIGHT, RETARGET, tower stats and
AI personality constants are passed into the worker. They are not a separately
maintained set of balance values. combat-rules.js provides shared targeting,
spell damage, movement slow, stun refresh, directional sight and Aether helpers.
V14 deck intelligence supplies coherent decks and mechanic-based counters.

The simulation is a model, rather than an execution of Three.js rendered
battles. It advances in 0.20-second steps; cooldown overshoot is retained within
one step so attack cadence does not accrue idle-time attacks. Ground navigation
uses committed bridge waypoints and reachable melee distance, rather than the
rendered game's A* obstacle search, congestion and soft separation. Headless AI
uses the same seven personality constraints, counters and synergy, with a
simpler visible-board deployment policy. Projectile impacts retain a scheduled
flight time calculated from live speed and source/target height. Visual effects,
animation and gradual model rotation are omitted. Acquisition is performed at
the headless step rate. These differences can change outcomes and should be
considered when confirming a balance hypothesis in a playable match.

No live card balance was changed. The full 6,262-character CARD_LIBRARY block,
with line endings normalized, is identical to V14. All existing locked tower,
regulation, overtime, tiebreak, Aether, targeting and special mechanic values
remain in game.js. A rare Cycle deck cost inconsistency was repaired: the
coherent draft constraint now enforces the already-tested 3.375 cost ceiling.
This changes deck selection, not card stats.

COMBAT AND BUDGET CHECKS

Each side starts with 5 Aether and caps at 10. Spending is allowed only for a
card currently in that side's hand when its actual cost is affordable. Played
cards re-enter the queue. Unspent generation above the cap is recorded as leak.

Generation is integrated across phase boundaries:
  elapsed 0–120 seconds: 1 / 2.8 Aether per second;
  elapsed 120–240 seconds: 2 / 2.8 per second;
  elapsed 240–300 seconds: 3 / 2.8 per second.

The regulation budget before leakage is:
  5 + 120/2.8 + 60/1.4 = 90.7142857143.
The full regulation plus overtime budget is:
  5 + 120/2.8 + 120/1.4 + 60/(2.8/3) = 197.8571428571.
Every accepted match checks spent <= generated budget, 0 <= remaining <= 10,
valid eight-card decks, finite telemetry and positive duration.

Regulation ends at exactly 180 seconds. Only a 0–0 regulation score starts
120 seconds of sudden-death overtime. An overtime tower kill ends the match.
If still 0–0, the lowest surviving tower HP determines the draining tiebreaker;
simultaneous equal minima use total tower HP, then the seeded coin flip. The
tiebreaker is 1–0; its artificial crown is not attributed to combat card damage.
Core destruction sets the score to three crowns. Card attribution uses the
actual increase from the previous score, avoiding double-counting a prior guard.

Melee troops cannot hit flying units unless the card actually canHitAir. Flying
troops ignore river crossing. Ranged units can fire across the river. Structure-
targeting troops select defensive buildings by reachable distance until they
enter attack range and lock. Ordinary troops retain targets within the actual
troop leash, and deployment pulls respect sight, legality, temporary forced
target duration and Crown Tower locks. Towers acquire troops, keep their target
through their live +0.7 retention distance and activate the core when damaged
or when a same-side guard falls. Defensive buildings acquire troops only,
start on their 0.35-second cooldown and lose maxHP/lifetime HP per second.

Frost applies its exact movement slow only on legal melee troop hits. Duration
refreshes; the percentage never adds to itself and attack speed is unchanged.
Storm Raven directs normal attacks at structures. Its independently timed aura
damages nearby ground and air troops and refreshes stun; aura can pulse while
Raven itself is stunned, matching live update order. Meteor's initial hit and
five scheduled zone pulses affect troops only. A target must still be within
the zone at a pulse. Arc projectiles hit their primary target and then each
legal nearby splash recipient. Nova and Bullet use actual troop/structure
damage, live circle radius and target-radius padding. Spells deploy immediately;
the live game has no extra combat deployment delay to reproduce.

CARD DENOMINATORS AND FORMULAS

A card appearance is one side's deck containing the card, even if never played.
Except for card WR, aggregate telemetry includes both mirror and non-mirror
appearances. A per-game card value means per deck appearance, not per overall
match and not per cast. This keeps a frequently picked card from looking more
effective merely because it has accumulated more total matches.

Pick% = appearances / number of included side decks * 100.
There are two decks per match and eight slots per deck, so the sum across all
14 card Pick% values is exactly 800% for any complete nonempty dataset or style /
archetype intersection. This is not merely a large-sample approximation. Card
type and sample filters intentionally show only a subset and can sum below 800%.

Clean N counts only a card present on exactly one side of a match. If both decks
contain that card, both appearances are excluded from its card WR signal. They
still contribute picks and measured telemetry. The mirror-exclusion counter is
the number of excluded appearances, rather than the number of unique matches.

score = wins + 0.5 * draws in clean samples.
Raw WR = score / clean N * 100; it is unavailable when clean N is zero.
Adjusted WR = (score + 12) / (clean N + 24) * 100.
The neutral Beta(12,12) prior pulls sparse samples toward 50%.

95% CI is the Wilson interval of empirical clean score / N, with z = 1.96:
  denominator = 1 + z²/N;
  center = (p + z²/(2N)) / denominator;
  half = z * sqrt((p*(1-p) + z²/(4N))/N) / denominator.
Bounds are clamped to 0–100%. Zero samples show the full 0–100 interval.
The Wilson interval describes raw observations; it is not a Bayesian posterior
interval around the adjusted point. Half-credit draws use a fractional-score
binomial approximation. Raw and adjusted WR stay separate in the UI and exports.

Uses/Game = casts / appearances.
Aether Spent/Game = actual cast costs / appearances.
Troop, Tower, Building Damage/Game = actual HP removed in that target class /
appearances. Requested damage beyond remaining HP is excluded from these totals.
Damage Taken/Game = HP lost to attacks and spells / appearances; building decay
is not incoming enemy damage.
Kills/Game = last-hit troop or deployable-building kills / appearances.
Deaths/Game = deployed entities destroyed / appearances, including decay.
Average Lifetime = summed lifetime of all deployed entities / entities spawned,
including surviving entities censored at match end. Swarm members count separately.
Average Placement Tile = signed mean arena X and Z per cast. These are world
coordinates across both teams; their Z mean can approach zero through mirrored
placements and should not be read as a recommendation to deploy on the river.
Average Kill Value = sum of defeated card cost / defeated swarm count, divided
by kills. Crown Towers have no replacement-Aether cost in this calculation.
Damage/Aether = (troop + tower + building HP removed) / actual Aether spent.
Tower Damage/Aether = Crown Tower HP removed / actual Aether spent.
Survival Rate = deployed entities still alive at match end / entities spawned.
Unit lifetime/survival is not an applicable measure of an instantaneous spell.
Opening-Hand Play Rate = opening-hand appearances played among that side's first
four casts / opening-hand appearances. Each eligible card is counted at most once.
First-Play Rate = occurrences as a side's first cast / card deck appearances.
OT Play Rate = casts during overtime / all casts.
Win-Condition Connection Rate = casts that caused Crown Tower HP damage / casts.
Each cast counts once, regardless of attacks or the number of swarm members.
Average Crown Contribution = actual crown increases attributed in proportion
to each card's cumulative damage to the fallen tower / card appearances. The
draining tiebreaker crown has no fabricated damaging-card attribution.

BUILDING, SPELL AND SPECIAL MECHANIC METRICS

Building damage prevented is actual incoming HP damage absorbed by the building.
It is a measured prevention proxy, not a claim that all that damage would have
otherwise hit a Crown Tower. Win-condition pulls count first acquisition of the
building by each structure-targeting entity before a different hard lock; repeat
scans do not count as new pulls. Lifetime utilization is actual building lifetime
used / its total nominal deployed lifetime capacity, expressed as a percentage.

Spell targets hit counts distinct target entities per cast, including newly
hit Meteor occupants later in the zone. Aether value estimates removed HP /
target maxHP times target card cost / swarm count; Towers have zero replacement
Aether cost. This is an HP-fraction proxy rather than a forecast of future
defense value. Overkill is requested spell damage above remaining target HP.
Spell damage per cast and tower damage use actual removed HP and cast count.
Spell kills are attributed to the damaging spell, including a lethal DoT pulse.

Frost slow uptime is affected time / tracked remaining lifetime of targets that
received Frost slow, beginning at first application. It measures observed
target duty time, not a flat assumed bonus to damage or attack speed.
Raven aura damage is separately tagged HP damage. Units stunned counts successful
stun applications; the same surviving unit can receive more than one pulse.
Average stun uptime uses affected / tracked target time after first application.
When overlapping sources refresh an effect, the latest source owns subsequent
measured status time, so the same target-time is not credited twice.
Meteor zone occupancy is average living enemy troop occupants integrated over
actual active zone seconds. It is a count, not a percentage. DoT ticks per cast
counts scheduled zone pulses (up to five), not targets multiplied by pulses.
Initial and DoT HP damage are tagged and reported separately. The maximum damage
to a stationary, sufficiently healthy single target is 262 + 5*40 = 462; actual
damage can be lower through death, movement, match end, or overlapping effects.

Zero denominators never produce NaN or Infinity. Empty estimates use zero or
explicit unavailable values according to the metric; they do not create samples.

MATCHUP MATRIX AND EMPIRICAL SYNERGY

Matchup cells compare the V14 stat-derived counter scores in opposite directions
and display the difference on -100 to +100. This is estimated mechanical
coverage, not a measured duel WR. Explanations are generated from actual legal
targeting, air status, range, HP, swarm count, spell damage, radius, structure
restriction, building decay/pulls, Frost slow and Raven's secondary aura.
Spell-vs-spell cells explain that neither spell is a damageable body. No match
observations or arbitrary duel outcomes are invented for this matrix.

Pair Games counts side decks containing both cards when the opposing deck does
not contain both. Joint mirror appearances are excluded from pair WR signals.
Half-credit draws are included. Individual single-card clean statistics exclude
their own mirrors independently, so the pair and card samples can differ.

Expected Baseline = mean of the two cards' Bayesian adjusted clean WRs.
Raw Pair WR = observed pair score / Pair Games * 100.
Shrinkage prior = 48 games centered on the expected baseline.
Adjusted Pair WR = (pair score + 48*baseline/100) / (Pair Games + 48) * 100.
Raw Delta = Raw Pair WR - Expected Baseline.
Synergy Delta = Raw Delta * Pair Games / (Pair Games + 48).
The displayed pair Wilson CI describes empirical pair outcomes. N is always
shown. A zero-sample pair has no empirical pair WR and no asserted positive
synergy. Shared deck composition, personality, opposing decks and card selection
can confound a pair delta; it measures association, not a causal interaction.

ARCHETYPES, PERSONALITIES, FILTERS AND ALERTS

All seven personality decks use coherent V14 deck generation. Sixteen percent
of draws explore shuffled legal compositions with troop, spell, anti-air and
win-condition coverage. Measured WR never feeds back into future deck sampling.
The Cycle draft ceiling is tested across 1,000 deterministic seeds. Every card
receives clean observations in the 10,000-game audit.

Deck archetype labels are actual composition classifications and can overlap.
Consequently archetype Pick% need not add to 100%, unlike the fixed card-slot
total. Archetype WR, crowns and duration come from observed side outcomes.
Best/weakest cards require at least 30 clean samples within the archetype;
best/worst opposing archetypes require at least 30 observations.

Card and pair filters use actual personality/archetype intersections. Archetype
reports condition on the personality selection, and personality reports
condition on the archetype selection. Their denominators use the included side
decks. History is explicitly whole-dataset cumulative snapshots; the chart's
100/500/1k/5k/10k/All selection changes the visible game span, not its population.

Alert minimum clean N is 100. A WATCH requires adjusted WR >=53% or <=47% and
the empirical Wilson interval entirely above or below 50%. CONCERN requires
N>=400 and CI lower >=52% or upper <=48%. STRONG SIGNAL requires N>=1,000 and
CI lower >=54% or upper <=46%. Supporting style slices require N>=100 and their
own Wilson interval excluding 50%. Spell alerts use actual damage metrics,
rather than claiming low unit survival. These are confidence-qualified model
signals, not proof that a live card is overpowered. Multiple comparisons and
deck-policy confounding remain relevant; the game does not automatically rebalance.

HISTORY AND PATCH COMPARISONS

Version, combat values, arena/match/grid/tower rules, personality configuration,
deck-policy revision and combat-model revision form the dataset identity. A
changed identity creates a new archive and never pools observations into the
old one. Every new archive retains its actual card/rule/style inputs. Resetting
the active dataset archives its prior run rather than silently erasing history.
When an old event-model fingerprint cannot be loaded into the current model,
its recorded clean N, score, appearances, uses, spending, damage and checkpoints
are projected directly from saved aggregates with their original identity.
They are not re-simulated or assigned the current model label. Unavailable new
mechanics remain blank; compatible historical style/archetype card slices retain
their own recorded denominator. Missing legacy building damage makes total-damage
patch comparison unavailable, rather than assuming unknown damage was zero.

Recent cumulative trend checkpoints are saved every 100 accepted games. Once
the history exceeds 2,000 points, older points are thinned while recent points
remain at 100-game resolution. Retained values are unchanged observations;
no smoothed or synthetic WR samples are inserted. All spans can include the
older, coarser checkpoints. This bounds worker cloning and disk-save growth.

Patch comparisons subtract adjusted WR, pick%, actual damage per appearance and
uses per appearance. The per-card Archetype Delta is the largest absolute change
in that card's conditioned archetype WR, with the archetype name and all
conditioned deltas available in the structured result. Clean N before and after
is retained. Differences between model revisions can reflect model changes,
rather than a card-balance change alone.

No reusable per-card V12.8, V12.10 or exact V14 session datasets were supplied.
Historical patch notes and audit summaries are preserved, but are not fabricated
into a dataset. The old local save key spans V12.10 through V14; if present, it
imports once as a limited archive with unknown exact session-version provenance.
Only its actually recorded card metrics are shown. Unknown pair, style, status,
history, damage-taken and patch-specific metrics stay unavailable. Requested
historical comparison entries exist and explain missing evidence, with N=0
instead of invented results or duplicated old counts.

PERFORMANCE AND FINAL SANITY RUN

The browser uses a dedicated Web Worker, yielding every four matches. Pause
preserves pending validation work; resume continues it. Background scheduling
is controlled separately from combat compute. Speed choices are 100, 250,
500 and MAX SAFE games/minute. Worker MAX SAFE uses actual recent compute
timings, reserves only 28% compute budget and caps at 2,000/minute; the controller
can lower this further when the page becomes busy. Live battle pause/throttle
is controlled by the game controller. Main UI updates and disk saves are bounded.

The validation script ran the actual current event engine for 10,000 seeded
matches on this machine. It does not claim those were rendered battles:
  seed 151515; model v15-event-2; fingerprint 47a565c8;
  elapsed wall time 72.19 seconds; approximately 8,311 games/minute unthrottled;
  invalid simulations 0; NaN/Infinity count 0;
  mirror-card appearances excluded 101,438;
  total card Pick% 800.000%; all 14 cards had clean observations;
  average game duration 280.78 simulated seconds;
  average actual Aether spent 172.99 per side;
  seven personality slices, ten archetypes and 91 distinct card pairs;
  100 actual trend checkpoints; serialized dataset approximately 1.51 MB.

The archived numeric V15_META_VALIDATION.json contains the actual full card
metrics, sample sizes, confidence intervals, style/archetype counts and signal
summaries. Browser responsiveness and the actual worker protocol are checked
separately from the command-line throughput benchmark. Session games/minute
excludes previously saved games after restoration, preventing a bogus huge rate.
