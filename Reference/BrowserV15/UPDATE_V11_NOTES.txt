Rift Crown Arena V11

1. Facing correction
- Blender-generated troop models use a dedicated forward-axis pivot.
- Player troops: back faces camera while advancing toward enemy side.
- Enemy troops: front faces camera while advancing toward player side.
- Fix covers Sky Manta and every other generated troop model without changing the model geometry.

2. Structure lock
- Tower-only troops hard-lock to a structure after entering attack range / beginning the attack.
- Newly placed buildings cannot pull them away after that lock.
- Lock clears only when the structure dies.
- Normal troops preserve Clash-style defender retargeting.

3. Guard Tower
- Guard Tower range = 10 tiles.

4. Live Meta Lab
- Target rate: 100 accelerated headless bot games per minute.
- UI refresh: once per second.
- Columns: Pick%, Win%, Uses/Game, Troop Damage/Game, Tower Damage/Game, Kills/Game, Deaths/Game, Damage/Aether, Balance Delta.
- Interactive sorting, filter, search, pause/resume, reset, CSV export.
- Simulation includes Beatdown/Aggro/Control deck tendencies, current card stats, basic synergies and counters.
