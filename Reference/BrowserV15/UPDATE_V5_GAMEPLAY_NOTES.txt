Rift Crown Arena V5 - Gameplay / HUD / Pathing Pass

Implemented:
- Compact in-match HUD so the player Core/King tower stays visible more often.
- Removed the in-match hover/inspect stat overlay that was blocking view.
- Added visible 1x1 arena placement tiles and tile-snapped troop deployment.
- Improved ground-unit pathing to behave more lane/bridge oriented like Clash Royale.
- Added soft same-team separation to reduce clumping/jamming.
- Added 3 enemy AI play styles with per-match style randomization:
  * Beatdown
  * Aggro
  * Control
- Improved troop health readability:
  * Thicker health bars
  * Numeric HP chips above units during combat/damage/movement
  * Better bar colors and frame contrast
- Kept tower numeric health plates working through one authoritative update path.
- Enhanced deployment / combat FX and some unit animation feel.
- Slightly adjusted battle camera to show more of the player side.
