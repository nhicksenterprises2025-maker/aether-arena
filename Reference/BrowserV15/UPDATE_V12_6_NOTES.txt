Rift Crown Arena V12.6 - Directional Sight Update

Target acquisition:
- All deployable combat cards now use directional sight.
- Forward 180 degrees: 8 tiles.
- Rear 180 degrees: 5 tiles.
- This replaces the old single aggro radius for live target acquisition.
- Air/ground targeting restrictions are still respected.
- Crown Tower hard-lock rules are unchanged.
- Attack range remains separate from sight range.

Archer Tower:
- Uses the same 8-tile front / 5-tile rear sight system.
- Its 7-tile attack range still caps how far it can shoot.

UI:
- Card details now show Front Sight and Rear Sight instead of the old Aggro Range value.
- Spells do not use sight because they are manually targeted area effects.
