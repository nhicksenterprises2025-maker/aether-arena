How to play

Destroy enemy Guard Towers for crowns. Destroy the enemy Core Tower to win instantly.

  • Drag a card onto your legal deployment tiles, or select it and click to deploy.
  • Each arena square is 1 tile. Guard Towers attack out to exactly 10 tiles.
  • Card attack range, aggro range, splash radius and spell radius are measured in arena tiles.
  • Destroy the enemy left or right Guard Tower to unlock a deployment pocket on that same enemy-side lane.
  • Archer Tower is a 3-Aether defensive building that attacks ground and air troops. Nova Flask can target anywhere.
  • Ground troops route through their lane bridge; flying troops cross the river directly.
  • Aether regenerates over time and doubles in the final minute.
  • Highest crown count wins at 0:00. Ties enter sudden overtime.
