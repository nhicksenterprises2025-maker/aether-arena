RIFT CROWN ARENA V14.0 — DECK ECOSYSTEM & STRATEGIC DEPTH
Completed 2026-10-08

SCOPE AND PRESERVATION
Built on the cleaned V13 Battle Intelligence project. The original V13 project
is preserved separately. No cards or game systems were removed. No new cards
were added and no live balance patch was introduced. Existing art, models,
arena, animation/FX, targeting, navigation, rules, profile, Meta Lab, Developer
Lab, launchers and historical notes are retained.

ADVANCED DECK WORKSHOP
- Full 14-card pool, eight unique draft slots, add/remove controls and separate
  inspection controls. Exactly-eight validation blocks incomplete saves/tests.
- Average Aether, cost curve, troop/spell/building counts, air and ground-only
  coverage, dedicated win conditions, splash/area and swarm counts.
- Average HP, direct DPS and range with visible aggregation notes. Swarm HP/DPS
  use complete deployments; spells do not dilute troop/building averages.
- Composition-derived profile, strengths and weaknesses update immediately.
- Existing visual identity preserved with a sticky action footer and mobile
  home profile/workshop layout.

ARCHETYPES AND SHARED ANALYTICS
- Pure deck analyzer derives Beatdown, Control, Cycle, Bridge Pressure, Split
  Lane, Air Pressure, Siege, Spell Control, Defensive and Hybrid labels.
- Multiple labels are supported; the primary profile remains readable.
- All mechanics come from the live library. Analytical calls do not mutate
  card values or gameplay state.
- Partial, corrupt, duplicate and unknown-card inputs are handled without NaN,
  misleading denominators or accidental prototype-property cards.

SYNERGY AND CARD INTELLIGENCE
- All 28 pairs in a complete deck receive explained 0–100 scores; the compact
  top-pair view expands to expose every pair.
- Scoring derives tank/support, anti-air, splash, cost, range, win-condition
  support, spell coverage, defense, statuses and swarms from actual mechanics.
  There is no manually enumerated table of every card pair.
- All 14 cards receive role, suggested uses, Best Against, Weak Against/Common
  Counters, Common Partners, Best Defensive Answer and Best Offensive Partner.
- Ground melee cannot directly counter flyers. Structure attacks cannot apply
  direct troop damage. Raven's ring, Frost refresh, splash, charge, building
  pull/lifetime and Meteor dwell contribute where mechanically appropriate.
  Spell survival is distinguished from cancelling a cast.

PRESETS AND TRAINING
- Five independent named presets, initially Deck 1 through Deck 5.
- Names are trimmed, sanitized and limited to 24 characters.
- Tabs select drafts. SAVE LOADOUT persists the selected preset and activates
  it for battle. Saving does not alter a running battle's hand/rotation.
- TEST DECK saves the selected draft and opens training. From an existing match
  it waits for queued saves before reload and training autostart.
- RESET DEFAULT changes the draft without deleting other saved presets.

SAVE COMPATIBILITY
- Existing profile/deck fields, browser keys and /api/save contract remain.
- Saves add deckPresets with five named decks and the active preset ID.
- Valid old V13 decks migrate into Deck 1. Missing/corrupt records recover
  defaults while preserving good named records.
- Local fallback, queued snapshots and late-hydration protection remain.
- A pending marker protects newer data after failed writes and retries it
  during hydration. Fetches have bounded timeouts.
- Manual Battle/Training and reload autostart wait for disk hydration.

MATCHUP PREVIEW AND AI
- Own deck profile is visible before battle on desktop and mobile.
- Opponent deck, composition advantage, lane plan, threats, counters, AI hand/
  cycle, strengths, weaknesses and win conditions appear only in Developer Lab.
- All seven V13 personalities generate coherent eight-card decks with suitable
  win conditions, support, defense, anti-air and spell coverage.
- The brain uses its own profile, legal counter scores, pair synergy, own
  rotation and existing personality tactics when defending or pushing.
- Counter readiness uses observed player plays/cycle. Player Aether is still
  estimated. Decisions do not read the player's private hand/Aether or the
  private matchup report.

CARD DETAILS
- Exact hit speed, movement, range, footprint and projectile speed to two
  decimals where needed. Full original stats and special mechanics retained.
- Buildings show stationary movement/count; spells label irrelevant fields
  N/A and show count, targeting and actual structure damage.
- Meteor maximum stationary damage 462 and structure damage 0 are explicit.
- Frost refresh/no stacking, Raven Ground + Air ring and Ram charge remain
  clear. Strategy notes expand beneath the primary grid.

BUGS FIXED DURING V14
1. Training/restart reload raced queued saves. Reload now waits for them.
2. Failed server writes let stale disk data replace newer local presets/profile
   on reload. Pending-marker protection keeps/retries local data.
3. Manual Battle/Training started before slow disk hydration and used a hand
   inconsistent with the restored preset. Both paths now await hydration.
4. Details rounded exact values such as 0.72, 1.35 and 1.45; fixed precision and
   filled missing spell/building fields without changing live stats.
5. Meta Lab excluded Raven aura from troop answers while direct structure DPS
   could be valued as troop DPS. Only actual aura/stun contributes now.
6. AI defense could select ground-only answers against air. Legal analytical
   target filtering prevents those unusable defenses.
7. Removed obsolete manual counter hints, superseded workshop handlers and an
   unused deck-average helper after shared implementations replaced them.
8. Fixed modal grid sizing and compacted mobile navigation so dialogs and the
   Training button fit the phone viewport instead of overflowing horizontally.

V13 CLEANUP FIXES RETAINED
- Stationary card selection, server deck persistence, profile/deck validation,

- Meteor fifth DoT tick (full 462 stationary damage).
