Rift Crown Arena V0.8.1 — Bridge Pathing + Loadout Builder

Bridge/pathing fixes
- Ground troops now commit to one bridge while crossing instead of switching bridge choices mid-crossing.
- Bridge crowd separation no longer pushes units sideways off the bridge route.
- Bridge constraint now clamps lateral position without resetting Z progress.
- Added short stuck detection/recovery near bridge mouths.
- Melee attack range now uses navigable bridge distance, preventing troops from stopping at the river edge because an enemy across the water looked close in straight-line distance.
- Ranged troops still use direct distance so they can legitimately shoot across the river.

Loadout builder
- Loadout now opens a dedicated deck builder instead of the card encyclopedia.
- Battle decks are exactly 8 unique cards.
- Click a current deck slot to remove it.
- Click a card in All Cards to add it.
- Save Loadout applies the deck to the next battle and persists it in local storage.
- Reset Default restores the default 8-card deck.
- Home Battle Deck and average Aether cost update immediately after saving.
- Cards tab still opens the full card-details encyclopedia.
