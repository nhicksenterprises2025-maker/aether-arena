How to play

Destroy enemy Guard Towers for crowns. Destroy the enemy Core Tower to win instantly.

  • Drag a card onto your legal deployment tiles, or select it and click to deploy.
  • Each arena square is 1 tile. Guard Towers attack out to exactly 10 tiles.
  • Card attack range, aggro range, splash radius and spell radius are measured in arena tiles.
  • Destroy the enemy left or right Guard Tower to unlock only the matching lane pocket between the river and the former Guard Tower approach. The pocket never reaches the enemy Core Tower.
  • Archer Tower is a 4-Aether defensive building. Vampire Bats are a 5-unit flying swarm. Frost Fang slows ground targets, Storm Raven is a flying structure-only win condition, and Meteor Shards is a large damage-over-time spell.
  • Core Towers stay dormant and do not fire until one of their own Guard Towers is destroyed.
  • Ground troops route through their lane bridge; flying troops cross the river directly.
  • Aether is 1× for the first two minutes, 2× for regulation minute three and overtime minute one, then 3× for the final overtime minute.
  • Regulation lasts 3:00. Only a 0–0 score unlocks 2:00 sudden-death overtime. If OT also ends 0–0, the live tower-HP tiebreaker begins.

V12.3

Meta Lab Accuracy Rebuild

The balance simulator now uses the real match economy and statistically corrected card win rates.
Real Aether Budget

The old model effectively spent enough Aether for a full five-minute match every simulation. V12.3 uses the real 3:00 regulation budget (~90.7 available Aether before leakage) and only adds overtime Aether when the simulated match actually reaches 0–0 overtime.

Mirror Bias Removed

When both decks contain the same card, that game no longer counts toward that card's balance win-rate signal. With 8-card decks from a small pool, mirror appearances were heavily pulling every card toward 50%.

Bayesian Win Rate

ADJ WIN% uses a neutral 24-game prior so tiny samples cannot immediately report extreme 30% or 70% results. The effect fades naturally as real simulation samples accumulate.

95% Confidence Interval

The table now shows a Wilson 95% interval and the non-mirror sample size. Yellow results have fewer than 30 clean samples and should not be treated as stable.

No Feedback Loop

Observed card win rate no longer changes how often the simulator drafts that card. The old feedback term could reinforce early noise and distort Pick%.

Comparable Card Math

Troops, buildings, spells, swarms, air targeting, splash and tower-only pressure are now evaluated with matchup-sensitive per-use models on comparable scales instead of the old raw-value formulas that massively inflated buildings and heavy spells.

V12.2

Pocket / Lane / Match Flow Hotfix

Deployment pockets now match the visible lane zone, center tiles choose their own lane, and regulation is 3:00.
Pocket Size Corrected

Destroying a Guard Tower unlocks only the matching lane rectangle between the river and the former Guard Tower approach. It no longer extends backward onto the enemy Core Tower.

Visible Zone = Legal Zone

The pocket overlay and placement validator now use the same exact X/Z bounds, so every highlighted pocket tile is legal and every unhighlighted enemy-side tile stays locked.

Center Lane Choice Fixed

A unit placed on the middle-left tile starts in the left lane; a unit placed on the middle-right tile starts in the right lane. The old random center-lane assignment is removed.

3:00 Regulation

If any Crown Tower has been taken by 3:00, regulation ends immediately. A tied non-zero crown score ends as a draw rather than opening the special 0–0 overtime.

2:00 0–0 Overtime

Only a 0–0 game after regulation receives two extra sudden-death minutes. The first tower destroyed during overtime immediately ends the match.

Aether Timeline

1× for the first two regulation minutes, 2× for regulation minute three and overtime minute one, then 3× for the final overtime minute. If OT also ends 0–0, the live tower-HP tiebreaker begins.

V12.1

Target Priority Hotfix

Normal troops notice enemy troops again without breaking Crown Tower hard-locks.
Troop Priority Restored

Normal troops continuously scan for reachable valid enemy troops inside their aggro range while traveling toward a Crown Tower.

Hard-Lock Timing Fixed

A Crown Tower is no longer hard-locked just because it was selected as a travel target. The permanent lock begins only when the attacker physically reaches attack range.

Bridge Combat Fixed

Bridge commitment now commits the route—not the target. A troop can notice and fight a valid enemy while still finishing its committed bridge crossing.

Deployment Pull Restored

Deploying a valid defender can redirect a normal attacker before it reaches Crown Tower attack range, including while that attacker is crossing a bridge.

Tower Lock Preserved

Once the attacker reaches a Crown Tower and begins the attack, troop placement, building placement and periodic target scans can no longer pull it away.

Win Conditions

Boulderback and Rambeast can still be pulled by a defensive building while traveling. Once they begin attacking a Crown Tower or building, that structure remains locked until destroyed.

System Changes

Original V12 systems; current match timing is superseded by V12.2 above.
Match Flow (superseded)

V12 originally used five-minute regulation. V12.2 now uses 3:00 regulation plus a conditional 2:00 overtime only when regulation ends 0–0.

Aether Rates

1× = +1 every 2.8s. 2× = +1 every 1.4s. 3× = +1 every ~0.933s. Maximum remains 10.

Live 0–0 Tiebreaker

If regulation and the conditional two-minute overtime both finish 0–0, combat stops and surviving Crown Tower HP drains evenly until the first tower falls. The result becomes 1–0.

Bridge Pathing

Radius-aware A* routing uses explicit bridge entrance, center and exit points, with congestion handling, obstacle clearance and stuck recovery.

Tile-Center Placement
