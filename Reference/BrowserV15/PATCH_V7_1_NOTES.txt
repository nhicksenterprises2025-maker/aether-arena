Rift Crown Arena V7.1 - Tower Lock / Aggro Redirect Hotfix

FIXED
- Normal troops are no longer permanently locked onto a tower once they begin attacking it.
- Deploying a valid defender inside their sight/aggro range can now pull them off the tower.
- Retarget checks happen faster while a normal troop is focused on a tower.
- Closest valid defender is prioritized, with a small bias for defenders placed in the attacker's path.
- Air-target restrictions are respected during retargeting:
  * Ground melee still cannot be pulled toward / attack flying units.
  * Ground ranged that can hit air can retarget to air units.
- Tower-only units are intentionally unchanged:
  * Boulderback stays locked onto towers.
  * Rambeast stays locked onto towers.
