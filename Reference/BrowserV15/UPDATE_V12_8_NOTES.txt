Rift Crown Arena V12.8 — Frost / Storm / Meteor Update

NEW CARDS
- Frost Fang: 5 Aether, 742 HP, 72 damage, 0.8s hit speed, 2.0 tiles/s, 1.1-tile melee range.
  Melee hits apply a 30% movement slow for 2.0s. The effect refreshes and does not stack.
- Storm Raven: 6 Aether flying ranged structure-only win condition. 1450 HP, 282 damage, 1.3s hit speed,
  1.18 tiles/s and 5-tile range. Its 2-tile electric ring pulses every 3 seconds for 82 troop damage and
  a 0.4-second stun to ground and air troops.
- Meteor Shards: 5 Aether, 4.5-tile radius, 151 initial troop damage plus 6 damage/second for five seconds
  to enemy ground/air troops that remain inside the zone.

VISUALS / ANIMATION
- Detailed Frost Fang fallback and Blender model with frost armor, saber fangs and crystal spines.
- Detailed Storm Raven fallback and Blender model with animated wings, electric core and ring geometry.
- New high-contrast card art for all three cards.
- Frost slow hit/footstep FX, visible slowed-target frost FX, Storm Raven pulse/stun FX, lightning projectile FX,
  and layered Meteor Shards falling-impact / danger-zone FX.

CLEANUP
- Persistent troop gravestone/corpse markers are removed. Unit groups are deleted shortly after the death FX window.

META LAB
- Meta storage version bumped so the old 11-card dataset cannot pollute the 14-card pool.
- Frost movement control, Storm Raven aura/stun, and Meteor Shards dwell-time DoT are explicitly modeled.
- 100 analytical games/min target retained.
