Systems:
- Ground navigation rebuilt with radius-aware A* routing.
- Bridges use explicit entry -> crossing -> exit waypoints.
- Heavy units such as Boulderback commit to a bridge and cannot bounce between bridge cells mid-crossing.
- Friendly bridge congestion/separation tuned to queue instead of sideways jamming.
- Structure-aware obstacle avoidance and stuck-route recovery added.
- Crown Tower targets are now absolute hard locks as soon as a troop commits to that tower; troop/building deployment cannot redirect it.
- Hard lock clears only when the locked tower is destroyed or the attacker dies.
- Placement now snaps to exact centers of 1x1 arena tiles.
- Troop preview highlights the complete selected tile.
- Building placement highlights every occupied footprint tile.
- Pocket deployment uses the same tile-center legality system.
