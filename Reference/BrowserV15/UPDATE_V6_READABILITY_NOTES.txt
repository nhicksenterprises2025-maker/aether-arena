Rift Crown Arena V6 - Readability Hotfix

Main fixes from the supplied battle screenshots:
- Rebuilt troop HP display as one large exact HP plate.
- Exact troop HP is always visible while the troop is alive.
- Removed the duplicate tiny plane-style troop health bar.
- HP plates dynamically scale by camera distance so far-away units stay readable.
- Rebuilt tower HP plates at higher resolution with larger exact HP text and thicker health meters.
- Tower HP plates also scale by camera distance.
- Player/enemy HP borders are strongly color coded.
- Simplified in-match card UI: large name, role, cost and artwork only.
- Removed tiny HP/DPS text and tiny stat ribbons from the in-match hand.
- Full exact card statistics remain in the deck/details screens outside combat.
- Slightly adjusted the battle camera so the player Core Tower sits higher above the hand.
- Reduced the darkness/height of the bottom HUD background.

V5 features retained:
- 3 AI play styles (Beatdown, Aggro, Control)
- bridge/lane pathing improvements
- soft unit separation
- placement tile grid and snapped placement
- drag/drop cards
- tactical AI push construction
